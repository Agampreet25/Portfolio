About

I’m an NLP engineer and a research intern with the SenticNet team at NTU SingaporeSenticNet works on affective computing and sentiment analysis at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.. My research sits in affective computingEmotion trajectories in latent space (Findings of EMNLP 2026), cross-domain sentiment transfer (IEEE TCSS), and domain-adaptive opinion mining (ICTACS)., modelling how emotion lives in language and how it shifts from one state to the next.

Whatever the domain, I do one thing. I take a high-dimensional signalEmotional trajectories, price series, smear images, MRI/PET scans, each reduced to a representation a model, and a human, can reason over. and find the representation that makes it legible, then insist the model can show its reasoningGrad-CAM, LIME, and Occlusion Sensitivity in the clinical work, and Monte Carlo dropout uncertainty in sarcoma risk stratification.. That work has reached IEEE, Springer, NeurIPS, ACL, ASH, and ASCOIEEE TCSS & OTCON, Springer Discover AI, NeurIPS Imageomics Workshop, Findings of EMNLP 2026, ASH 2025, ASCO 2026..

I’m also the founder of Symbianthro Labs. I mentor students through the full arc of a research project, from first question to accepted paper, and Symbianthro is how I want to scale that by bridging the gap between students who want to do research and the mentors who can guide them.

Selected Research

Learning Structured Emotional Dynamics in Latent Space via Trajectory Modeling and Controlled Transitions

Findings of ACL · EMNLP 2026· Published · 2026
A. Saini, B. Abusalameh, R. K. Singh, E. Cambria
Models emotion as trajectories through a learned latent space, with controlled transitions between emotional states.

Transfer Learning for Cross-Domain Sentiment Classification via Domain Adaptation

IEEE Trans. Computational Social Systems· Published · 2025
R. K. Singh, A. Saini, E. Cambria, A. Kumar
WBiGe fuses static and contextual embeddings through a hierarchical BiGRU encoder and lightweight CNNs, with class-aware domain alignment and dual-level attention driving continuous adaptation across domains.
92.75% peak accuracy

Cross-Domain Knowledge Adaptation Using Iterative Scaling and Language Models

ICTACS 2024 · IEEE· Published
R. K. Singh, P. Sinha, A. Saini, M. K. Sachan
A cross-domain opinion-mining model that isolates and transfers domain-invariant sentiment signals, using tf-idf separation and iterative scaling to bridge source and target under scarce annotation.
81.05% accuracy

Forecasting Gold Price using Hybrid Deep Neural Network LSTM-Autoencoder

Discover AI · Springer· Published · 2025
A. Saini, R. K. Singh, P. Sinha
A hybrid LSTM-Autoencoder trained on 24 years of market data captures long-horizon structure while suppressing overfitting, outperforming LSTM, CNN, and LSTM-CNN baselines across the board.

Performance Evaluation of Machine and Hybrid Deep Learning Models for Bitcoin Value Forecasting

IEEE OTCON 2025· Published
A. Saini, R. K. Singh, T. Choudhury, V. Jain
A rigorous benchmark of tree ensembles against deep models for minute-wise Bitcoin forecasting, isolating the decisive role of feature engineering on structured financial data.
MAE 14.11R² 0.99998

Financial Time Series Prediction on Apple Stocks Using Machine and Deep Learning Models

IEEE ICECET 2024· Published
A. Saini, N. Singh, R. K. Singh, M. K. Sachan
Linear Regression, CNN, and LSTM contrasted on Apple stock data, mapping the real trade-off between temporal expressivity and interpretability in live forecasting.

A Hybrid Wavelet-GNN-LSTM Framework for Cryptocurrency Forecasting

IEEE CICN 2025· Published
A. Saini, R. K. Singh, P. Sinha
Wavelet decomposition, graph neural networks for inter-asset structure, and LSTM temporal modeling combine into a single pipeline for forecasting volatile, coupled crypto markets.
F1 0.9671R² 0.9996

Field-Level Visual Explanations for Leukocyte Classification: Interpretability in Peripheral Smear Diagnostics

ASH Annual Meeting 2025· Published
M. Saini, A. Saini, J. S. Negi, S. S. M. Vemula
A MobileNetV2 classifier for full-field peripheral smears, augmented with Grad-CAM, LIME, and Occlusion Sensitivity so every prediction traces back to clinically meaningful morphology.
97% mean accuracy

Automated Diagnosis of PCOS: A Deep Learning Approach Using Ultrasound Data

Global AI Summit 2025· Published
C. Gupta, L. Agarwal, S. Sharma, R. K. Singh, A. Saini, J. S. Negi
PCause pairs GAN-based synthesis, PCA reduction, and DenseNet-121, then ships as a cross-platform app for early, scalable PCOS screening in underserved settings.
93.4% test accuracy

Uncertainty-Aware Multimodal Imaging for Lung Metastasis Risk Stratification in Extremity Soft-Tissue Sarcoma

ASCO Annual Meeting 2026· Published
M. Saini, A. Saini, E. Cambria
MRI and FDG-PET fused at the representation level with Monte Carlo dropout uncertainty; deferring the least-certain cases measurably lifts retained accuracy in rare-cancer decision support.

Artificial Intelligence for Spatial Transcriptomics: A Scoping Review of Architectures and Models

NeurIPS 2025 Workshop · Imageomics· Published
A. Saini, S. Gandotra, A. Kumar, P. Dutta, T. Ghosal
A systematic map of 40+ deep learning frameworks for spatial transcriptomics, organized by the biological tasks their representations serve and the open road toward foundation models for spatial biology.

WellGuide: Bridging AI and Medicine with Biomedical LLMs for Enhanced Health Support

ICPDCSDT 2025· Published
S. Sharma, A. Saini
A healthcare support system built on BioMedGPT, weaving clinical NER, conversational memory, and structured patient workspaces into a privacy-conscious biomedical assistant.
