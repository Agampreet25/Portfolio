About

I’m a Computer Science graduate from the University of Petroleum and Energy Studies. My work lives wherever data is messy and the truth is buried — and it spans an unusually wide rangeEvidencePublications across sentiment transfer, gold & crypto forecasting, hematologic imaging, sarcoma risk, and spatial transcriptomics. of domains held together by a single method.

Across all of it I do one thing: take a high-dimensional signalEvidencePrice series, smear images, MRI/PET scans, gene-expression maps — each reduced to a representation a model, and a human, can reason over. and find the representation that makes it legible — then insist the model can show its reasoningEvidenceGrad-CAM, LIME, and Occlusion Sensitivity in the clinical work; Monte Carlo dropout uncertainty in sarcoma risk stratification..

That work has reached IEEE, Springer, NeurIPS, ASH, and ASCOEvidenceIEEE TCSS & OTCON, Springer Discover AI, NeurIPS Imageomics Workshop, ASH 2025, ASCO 2026.. Beyond publishing, I mentor undergraduate researchers through the full arc of a project — from first question to accepted paper.
